Classification Rules

Domain Classification

    1. Primary Domain: The domain requiring the deepest knowledge to solve the question
    1. Secondary Domain: Domain providing context or auxiliary concepts
    1. NO DOUBLE-COUNTING: Marks assigned to primary domain only
    1. Interdisciplinary Rule: If a question genuinely spans two domains (e.g., PCA = Linear Algebra + ML), assign primary based on the core mathematical operation required. PCA → Linear Algebra (eigen-decomposition is the engine). Naive Bayes → Probability (Bayes theorem is the engine).

Question Type Classification

    1. MCQ: Single correct option, negative marking (1/3 for 1-mark, 2/3 for 2-mark)
    1. MSQ: One or more correct options, ALL must be selected, no partial credit, no negative marking
    1. NAT: Numerical answer entered via virtual keypad, no negative marking

Difficulty Scale (1-7)

  1. Trivial — Direct recall / single formula application
  1. Easy — Standard textbook problem, no twists
  1. Moderate — Multi-step, standard concept, ~2-3 min
  1. Moderate-High — Conceptual twist, requires insight
  1. High — Non-standard variant, multi-concept synthesis
  1. Very High — Complex multi-domain, heavy computation
  1. Extreme — Novel variant, notation-heavy, trap-prone, ~4-6 min

Cognitive Level (Bloom's Taxonomy Adapted)

    1. Recall: Definition, formula, fact retrieval
    1. Comprehension: Explain concept, identify applicable principle
    1. Application: Execute standard procedure on given input
    1. Analysis: Decompose problem, identify hidden structure, compare alternatives
    1. Synthesis: Combine concepts across domains, derive novel solution

Solving Time Estimation

| Type | Marks | Estimated Time | |------|-------|----------------| | MCQ | 1 | 0.5 - 2 min | | MCQ | 2 | 2 - 4 min | | MSQ | 1 | 2 - 3 min | | MSQ | 2 | 3 - 5 min | | NAT | 1 | 2 - 4 min | | NAT | 2 | 3 - 6 min |

ROI Formula

ROI = (Expected Marks Yield) / (Hours Required to Master)
    1. Expected Marks Yield = Historical frequency × Average marks per appearance × Probability of appearance in 2027
    1. Hours Required to Master = Estimated study hours for Level 3+ mastery (timed solving)
    1. Does NOT predict exact future marks — used only for preparation prioritization

Trend Methodology

    1. Three years (2024-2026) is a VERY SMALL SAMPLE
    1. Trends reported as: "Observed in 2024-2026" NOT "GATE has permanently shifted"
    1. Trajectory labels: STABLE, INCREASING, DECREASING, VOLATILE, INSUFFICIENT DATA
    1. No extrapolation beyond observed data
    1. Confidence levels: MAXIMUM, HIGH, MODERATE, LOW

Confidence Levels for Claims

    1. CONFIRMED: Verified against Tier 1 source directly
    1. PLAUSIBLE: Consistent with Tier 1 evidence, minor discrepancies possible
    1. WEAKLY_SUPPORTED: Based on repository's own methodology, not official classification
    1. UNSUPPORTED: Asserted without evidence, or evidence contradicts
    1. FALSE: Directly contradicted by Tier 1 evidence
    1. INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE: Cannot determine with available data

HTML Generation

HTML files (index.html, solutions_1.html, etc.) should be generated from structured data via templates, not hand-edited. This ensures:

    1. Consistency across documents
    1. Reproducibility
    1. Single point of truth
    1. Easy updates when new data arrives

Incorporating Future GATE Papers

When GATE 2027 paper is released:

  1. Download official question paper and answer key (Tier 1)
  1. Parse into questions_2027.json using same schema
  1. Run validation against 2027 syllabus
  1. Update trends.json with 2027 data point
  1. Recompute ROI model
  1. Update archetype frequencies
  1. Generate new strategy recommendations
  1. Archive 2027 analysis in research/2027_analysis.md

Limitations & Uncertainty Awareness

  1. Sample Size: 3 years × 65 questions = 195 data points. Statistical significance is LOW.
  1. Memory-Based Questions: 2024-2026 data in repository may contain memory-based reconstructions, not official papers.
  1. Classification Subjectivity: Domain/cognitive classification involves judgment. Inter-rater reliability not measured.
  1. Syllabus Changes: 2027 syllabus revised. Historical patterns may not transfer.
  1. No Predictive Validity: ROI scores and trend labels are preparation aids, not mark predictors.
  1. GA Fixed: General Aptitude always 15 marks / 10 questions — not informative for DA-specific strategy.
